04 · Pipeline

Deterministic first; Apertus only points, and only on the residue

The census and every repair route run without a model. The converter reads OPD metadata, then frozen German, French and Italian rules, and offers the documents the rules leave open to Apertus, which may pick one printed date or null. Every output is validated against schema v0.1: spans re-checked against the text, typed nulls instead of blanks.
