# Technical report — Gegenprobe: one schema and a converter for Swiss parliamentary answers

**Track 2A — OpenParlData** · Online · Team 444 — Roman (+ Claude Code agents, §7) · Evidence site https://gegenprobe.mi11ion.io · **Maturity:** census and Bern repair lists = *ready for OPD to apply* (needs its choice on the document hash); source-adapter lists = *audited prototype*; nightly job = *deployable prototype* (one real night on a CI runner; not yet at OPD); rules-only converter = *tested prototype*; review mode, whole and virtual affairs = *feasibility study*; schema v0.1 = *draft*.

## 1. Summary

**Verdict.** A common schema is feasible with date roles and typed nulls; the converter fills what documents print: the answer date in depth, 7 fields on whole affairs, per-question Q/A pairs, people, recommendation and reasoning (P5); only the type runs unattended (the *unattended bar*: a 95% lower bound ≥ 0.80). Of 17 fields, 12 are shared (meaning tested for 1, the other 11 by definition), 5 shared with a role split, all measured ("answer date" is decided, dispatched or registered), 3 concepts body-specific; **4 fields are feasible from metadata, 13 partial, 0 need a different concept** (§5). Every value carries its source (page and character span, or the OPD record; an Apertus value is printed text by construction); categorical suggestions are document-level; a missing value is a typed null that says why. By default frozen DE/FR/IT rules run without a model and no outside service beyond OPD's exports and the parliaments' own sites; review mode adds one model endpoint the operator sets.
**The cross-parliamentary result.** OpenParlData (OPD) publishes the affairs of 89 Swiss parliaments with affairs in scope (it scrapes 90). Of 91,268 interventions whose events record a written government answer, **16,749 (18.4%) have no answer text in OPD**; 1,422 were answered orally (1,016 Valais oral-answer stubs, 406 question-time answers), so **15,327 written answers are missing: 17.1% of the 89,846 answered in writing** (2024–25: 1,488 of 12,632, 11.8%), each typed by cause. Bern 2024–25: 567 of 593 written answers lack text, most in OPD but unlinked; Neuchâtel 2024–25: 161 of 162 are not in OPD. OPD confirmed the Bern linking bug and that our relink list is the workaround until its fix ships (correspondence, 10 Oct 2026; a published excerpt awaits OPD's consent). **Deterministic routes now propose repairs for 9,720 of the 16,749** (was 3,107): source adapters for 50 parliaments verify 6,613 more; a blind audit of 300 random ones finds 297 answers, 0 wrong, 3 unclear (precision 0.990, Wilson 0.971–0.997); 5,007 written answers still need adapters (was 10,800).
**Where Apertus earns its place** (§6). Not by beating the same model asked freely (P2 53 vs 54 of 62, re-parsed; P6 59 vs 62 of 68, registered), but by finding what rules miss and abstaining where nothing is printed: on 15 parliaments no rule was written for it finds 24/29 answer dates, the rules 2/29 (ahead in 6 parliaments, behind in none, p = 0.031), and dates 4 of 33 answerless documents where the latest printed date dates 31; on 8 more (P6) rules, then Apertus find 31/34, rules alone 20/34 (11:0, p = 0.001); it fills whole-affair answer dates (22/27 → 27/27; 20/35 → 31/35) and doubles submitter extraction (P5: 12/40 → 24/40). A model from another vendor relabelled the gold blind: no registered decision changes in P1, P2, P6; in P5 too noisy to judge.

## 2. Architecture

1. **Census** (`census/`): streams the OPD exports once into a per-affair *answer-link status* (`attached | agenda | parentless | absent | image_only | oral`), the feasibility matrix and a comparability measure.
2. **Repair** (`repair/`): deterministic routes per cause, triple-checked (affair number or title, answer heading, date against OPD's answer event), as lists keyed on OPD ids: Bern relinks and fetches, source adapters for 50 parliaments (`repair/adapters/`); a nightly job (`deploy/nightly/`) re-runs them.
3. **Converter** (`convert/`, `qa/`): (i) OPD metadata and events, (ii) frozen DE/FR/IT anchor rules, (iii) the Apertus closed-set pointer on the residue. Modes: `rules_only` (default, no model) and `review` (suggestions with page, span and margin, flagged `needs_review`).
4. **Schema and validator** (`schema/`, `validate/`): Pydantic schema v0.1, normative; every field is a value with provenance `{value, role, page, char_start, char_end, quote, route, rule, model_build, θ, margin}` or a *typed null* (`not_in_document | unlinked | image_only | oral | text_failed | present_unanchored | no_rules_for_body | not_applicable`); `QAItem` pairs question *n* with its answer passage, filled by `qa/` (P5); validation uses the Pydantic models.

## 3. Use of Apertus

**Closed-set pointer** (`convert/pointer.py`, prompts `src/prompts/{de,fr,it}`). The document (first 12,000 characters) and the question go in the user message, the assistant turn is prefilled with a JSON prefix, and `null` and every distinct date printed in the document are scored as continuations by summed log-probability (`prompt_logprobs`). Null if `lp(null) − logsumexp(lp(dates)) > θ`, else the best date, mapped back to its span. θ does not transfer across builds (spike F2: a θ fitted on RLVR scores 0.25 on the final build), so it is fitted per build on dev documents and frozen: θ = 10.40 for the Public AI build (AUROC 0.99, leave-one-out 124/127 [93.3, 99.2]). The same form picks labels, OPD affair numbers and, in `qa/`, the question a paragraph answers, the recommendation sentence or the submitter's printed name. **Why a pointer** (spikes, `results/report/cited_spikes.json`): asked freely, Apertus dates 15/15 (8B) and 14/15 (70B) Bern interventions that have no answer; a null-allowed prompt fixes the 70B (0/15), not the 8B (14/15). A reader v2 (answer-present gate, then the gold guide's decision-before-letter-date rule) did not beat that prompt in P6 (§6).

**Which Apertus, which size.** Unless marked 8B, Apertus numbers are the **70B** weights, which Public AI serves as `swiss-ai/apertus-v1.5-8b`; inferred, not documented: the served fingerprint (`tp8-pp2-…`, tensor-parallel 8 × pipeline-parallel 2) and the behaviour point to the 70B (cited: 760/792 greedy outputs byte-identical to the 70B name; MMLU 84.4% vs 70.8% for the v1 8B route). The response `model` is checked on every call (the `-70b` name answers prefills with Qwen-SEA-LION, 3/3), and every cached P1/P2 answer comes from one served build (`results/eval/served_models.json`, D10). The **8B** is `swiss-ai/Apertus-v1.5-8B` as a community text-only export quantised to 4 bits by a community member (`Colby/apertus-v1.5-8b-text-Q4_K_M-GGUF`, sha256-pinned; not an official Swiss AI release; `docs/runbook.md` "Supply chain"), run with llama.cpp on one 4-vCPU machine, own θ = 16.73 (exploratory arms, D2/D3). On P1 it is less accurate than the 70B (pointer and cascade McNemar p = 0.0051 and 3.6 × 10⁻⁵) and dates 25 of 90 answerless documents (70B: 7); its residue suggestions are right 7/34 (70B 9/16). On the 52 P2 documents reachable from CI (D2-6) it is behind the 70B (2 vs 10 documents, p = 0.039); it finds 15 of 21 answer dates (70B 16, rules 2, the ungated latest printed date 14) but dates 11 of 31 answerless documents (70B 4). Its fast scorer failed both registered parity gates and passed a replacement gate written before any 8B arm ran (D9). The 8B on P6 is running on CI. **Recommendation:** `rules_only` unattended; for review suggestions the 8B as the measured sovereign option (a reviewer rejects more), the 70B for accuracy on infrastructure the operator chooses. Review mode refuses a served build θ was not fitted on: the first response from another fingerprint stops the run (exit 3) before any suggestion is attached (`docs/runbook.md` "Review mode in operation").

**Cost** (`results/report/cost_end_to_end.json`, one core, no GPU). Fetch, extraction and checks: about 2.1 s per document, plus 6.9 s per OCR page; the rules process 480 documents per second (0.5 CPU-hours for all 902,754 documents in OPD's export). Review mode adds the pointer on the residue (97 of 154 held-out documents): 3.5 calls and 5,905 billed prompt tokens per residue document on the 70B, USD 0.59 per 1,000 residue documents at Public AI's list price; the 8B takes 180 s per document on 4 vCPUs, 50 machine-hours per 1,000. At ten times OPD's volume: about 5 CPU-hours for the rules, about USD 3,357 for review mode on the 70B.

## 4. Data and operations

OPD bulk exports (snapshot 6 Oct 2026; OPD's API robots.txt disallows `/v1/`), Bern open data, Fedlex, parliament sites and APIs, fetched politely (≥ 1.2 s per host; robots.txt per RFC 9309, incident in §5). `make run` and the nightly unit run the image read-only, as a non-root uid, with `--cap-drop ALL` and `no-new-privileges`; PDFs are capped at 50 MB, each pdftotext/tesseract call at 300 s, each document at 600 s, every hit a typed failure row; failed alerts are re-sent the next night. **One real night** (`results/monitor/night_real_38062530586/`): under systemd on a GitHub-hosted 4-vCPU runner against OPD's live exports, 83 min (32 of them reading the exports: 6.2 GB streamed, 198 MB of single exports stored; about 50 the first night's triple check of all 2,508 Bern rows), 28 min of CPU, 2,078 MB peak; it reproduced the Bern list on live data (2,459 relink, 49 fetch) and raised no alert (a first night has no previous state for drop and trend alerts). A two-night Docker run is scheduled. Inside OPD (`GP_EXPORTS_DIR`) a night with no changed files took 96.8 s.

## 6. Evaluation: seven pre-registrations

**Design.** Seven tests: **P1**, 154 held-out documents from parliaments the rules were written for; **P2**, 62 documents from 15 parliaments no rule was written for; **P3**, 29 whole affairs; **P3b**, its pre-registered replication on 36 fresh affairs; **P4**, affairs rebuilt from minutes; **P5**, Q/A pairs, people, recommendation and reasoning on 40 fresh affairs (registered in `eval/prereg6`); **P6**, an answer-date reader v2 on 68 fresh documents (`eval/prereg7`). Each froze code, prompts, θ and the scorer by sha256 before any label was committed: the LOCK commit precedes the labels by 6 min 0 s (P1), 2 min 28 s (P2), 3 min 37 s (P3), 4 min 3 s (P4) by commit date, an order GitHub's clock confirms (`results/eval/lock_server_times_all.json`); for P3b, P5 and P6 the LOCK precedes the draw. P1's frozen rules were committed with its sample (D12): P1 is held out by protocol, P2 is the clean out-of-sample test of the rules. Unit (P1, P2, P6): one document's answer date, strict. Arms on identical inputs: **rules**; **pointer** (Apertus on every document); **cascade** (rules, then the pointer on the residue = every review suggestion accepted); **naive** free generation, plain and null-allowed, same weights.
**Gold, checked by another vendor.** No human labelled a gold value, by design: two blind Claude annotators (Opus, Sonnet), a third breaks ties, quotes verified by code, raw annotations committed. `Qwen-SEA-LION-v4-32B-IT` (AI Singapore, Qwen family; neither Anthropic nor Apertus) relabelled all 216 P1 and P2 documents blind from the same guide and text (post hoc, D14/D2-7; 216 calls, USD 0.14), and P5 and P6 as registered. Exact answer date: P1 134/154 (κ 0.80), P2 56/62 (κ 0.87). No registered decision changes when only the documents where both vendors agree are kept or Qwen's labels replace the gold: P2-H2 is 22 vs 0 under the gold, 21 vs 0 and 23 vs 1. It reads the same text, so it is no human audit. P1-H1 (null safety) rests on contested documents: 6 of the 7 values fall on documents OPD types as reports; without the 24 reports it is 1/72 (upper 7.5%), where both vendors agree 2/84, under Sonnet's labels 5/88. Non-LLM checks: OPD's event matches 37 of 44 checkable P1 dates (5 of the other 7 are 1–5 days apart: decision vs dispatch); Ticino titles 20/20; P3 answer dates 18/21; P2: 6 of 29 checkable exactly (6/6 agree), 19/29 face a non-LLM bound. **Statistics** (post hoc, D10): over the 9 registered confirmatory tests of P1–P4 and P3b all 4 held conclusions survive Holm (P2-H2 3.8 × 10⁻⁶) and BH; the registered lenient metric changes no decision; document-level p-values ignore clustering by parliament, so per-parliament results stand beside them. Deviations: `eval/prereg*/DEVIATIONS*.md`; D8: the Opus tie-breaker sided with Opus in 36/41 P1 ties, 35 on the recommendation only.

**What the tests say.** Apertus earns its place where layout varies and no rule exists: it finds what rules miss (P2: 22:4 documents against the rules; P6: rules → Apertus 11:0; whole-affair answer dates in P3/P3b), doubles submitter extraction (P5), and abstains where nothing is printed (P2). On P1 the rules alone match rules + Apertus (Branch B), so the default runs no model; in P4 Apertus added no significant decisions; the rules are incomplete too (P1 recall 47/64, P4 99/120). **Accuracy is not the pointer's argument:** asked freely with a null-allowed prompt, the same Apertus is as accurate (P2 re-parsed 54/62 vs 53/62, discordant 1 vs 0, p = 1; P6 62 vs 59). The closed-set form guarantees a printed value with page and span (32/32 on P2; no pointer arm returned an unprinted date, the plain prompt invented 24 in P1 and 5 in P6), and its margin orders the review queue (strong band 26/33 right, weak 3/11, post hoc); the free prompt quotes its date verbatim for 17/32 values. Writing the gold guide's decision-before-letter-date rule into the prompt lifted every Apertus arm on P1 dev (in-sample; free prompt 116/127 → 125/127).
