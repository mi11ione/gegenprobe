## 1. Summary

**Can one schema hold Swiss parliamentary business? Yes, if each field says which role its value plays** (e.g. "answer date" = the government's decision, its letter or the submission date). Asked of interventions, OPD's five intervention types: 198,594 of its 325,691 affairs; parliamentary initiatives (5,817) and government business (37,864) are outside the tested scope. *Shared:* one schema of 17 fields for communal, cantonal and federal business; every value carries its role and source (page and span, or the OPD record), every gap a typed reason. *Needs roles:* **10 fields**, by source of the split 4 parliamentary practice, 4 partly OPD's tagging (which OPD can correct), 2 open; 2 are fragile (the split holds under one of P8's two checks). Post hoc, in P10's gold from 15 parliaments none of the 197 people needed the guide's open `other` role (0/197). *Body-specific:* **1 field and 2 constructs**: the submission date (Vernier and Lausanne print theirs nowhere); oral answers, councils that publish only minutes. *Untested:* **6 fields**, defined once in v0.1; meaning is measured for the other 11: 4 on OPD's labels, 6 on the documents of 66 affairs from 11 parliaments (P8), 1 (type) on both.

**Delivered:** repairs for 9,720 answers keyed on OPD ids, every one from model-free rules; schema v0.2 with the roles; a converter that gives every value its page and span; a nightly job. Apertus only picks values printed in the document, and code locates them (70B weights via the Public AI gateway; local 8B GGUF on CPU). **Runtime external services:** none by default (public sources only); Apertus review mode sends document text to the Public AI gateway, or nothing with the local 8B.

1. **Schema.** P8 finds roles in authorship, co-signatory lists, the instrument behind a harmonised type and the language (text, OPD's tag and the affair's can differ). The converter fills v0.2's roles with fixed rules (75 of its 105 records carry printed authors with their role); on 5 parliaments its rules never saw, the language is right on 60/60 documents and author (name, role) pairs reach precision 39/49 at recall 39/137 (exploratory). Tables, annexes, images, scanned pages and signatures are marked by page range (66 of 105 converter records carry one); in a blind check of 30 documents (108 pages; exploratory, in-sample for the detector) tables are found at page precision 12/12 and recall 12/15, annexes at 6/6 and 6/6.

3. **Apertus** earns its place choosing the right value among printed candidates. *The answer date:* without rules it finds **24/29** answer dates, the rules 2/29 (P2, registered; by parliament the gain is [-0.02, 0.54]), a generic keyword rule 16/29 (post hoc); on the review queue 95.8% of its promotions are answers whose printed answer date passes the date check, 73.2% for a no-model exact-date match (P7-H4, registered, weighted). *Who is an author* (P10, registered: recall +23.4 points over the rules, precision non-inferior; not significantly better than OPD's list, H3a): post hoc, the gain over the rules comes from the model-free candidate finder plus document order (52/141, F1 0.41; Apertus 50/141, F1 0.42), and Apertus adds precision deciding who is an author (+7.3 points [0.8, 18.2], uncorrected). Where rules exist it adds about one document (P1: cascade 139/154, model-free cascade 138/154, post hoc).

## 2. Architecture

**Census** (`census/`) streams the OPD exports once into a per-affair *answer-link status*, the feasibility matrix and a comparability measure; a derived layer (`census/corrected.py`) places every registered gap row once the repair routes have looked (Fig. 1). **Repair** (`repair/`): deterministic routes per cause, each row checked (affair number or title, answer heading, date against OPD's answer event), as lists keyed on OPD ids; a nightly job (`deploy/nightly/`) re-runs the Bern route. **Converter** (`convert/`, `qa/`): OPD metadata and events, then frozen DE/FR/IT anchor rules, then the Apertus closed-set pointer on the residue; modes `rules_only` (default, no model) and `review` (suggestions with page, span and margin, flagged `needs_review`); `python -m gegenprobe affair --opd-affair <id>` turns one OPD affair into one validated v0.2 record, with OPD's text spans (`opd_text_span`) and Q/A pairs. **Schema and validator** (`schema/`, `validate/`): Pydantic schema v0.1, normative; a value carries its role, page, span, quote hash, route and rule, a null its typed reason (`data/schema/null_reasons.json`); `QAItem` pairs question *n* with its answer passage; author roles are checked for consistency (a sole author never next to joint authors; 2 records repaired).

## 3. Use of Apertus

**Closed form only.** Apertus never writes a value. (i) *Verifier* (P7, `repair/adapters/adjudicate.py`): for an adapter review row it answers "does this document print the executive's answer to this intervention (number, title)?" (yes vs no scored after a JSON prefix, θ_A = 8) and picks the answer date among the printed dates or none (θ_B = 10); a row is promoted only if both pass and the date passes the adapters' own date rule. (ii) *Pointer* (`convert/pointer.py`, prompts `src/prompts/{de,fr,it}`): the document (first 12,000 characters) and the question in the user message, the assistant turn prefilled with a JSON prefix, `null` and every distinct printed date scored by summed log-probability; null if `lp(null) − logsumexp(lp(dates)) > θ`. Code locates every span (`pointer.locate`). θ does not transfer across builds, so it is fitted per build on dev documents and frozen (θ = 10.40, AUROC 0.99). **Why closed:** asked freely, Apertus dates 15/15 (8B) and 14/15 (70B) Bern interventions that have no answer.

**Which model.** Evaluation used **Apertus v1.5 70B weights served by Public AI** under the name `swiss-ai/apertus-v1.5-8b` (inferred from the served fingerprint `tp8-pp2-…` and 760/792 greedy outputs byte-identical to the `-70b` name); the response `model` is checked on every call. The **8B** (`swiss-ai/Apertus-v1.5-8B`, a community 4-bit GGUF, sha256-pinned) runs locally on CPU with its own θ = 16.73 (exploratory): on P2's unseen parliaments the registered P2-H2 test over the rules gives 13 vs 0; its two-stage reader v2 cuts dates on answerless documents from 18/34 to 5/34 (P6). **Recommendation:** `rules_only` unattended; for suggestions the 8B where no text may leave the machine, the 70B for accuracy on infrastructure the operator chooses. Review mode stops (exit 3) on a served build θ was not fitted on.

**Cost** (`results/report/cost_end_to_end.json`, one core, no GPU). Fetch, extraction and checks about 2.1 s per document plus 6.9 s per OCR page; the rules process 480 documents per second (0.5 CPU-hours for all 902,754 documents in OPD's export; at ten times OPD's volume about 5 CPU-hours). Review mode: USD 0.59 per 1,000 residue documents on the 70B; 180 s per document on 4 vCPUs on the 8B, so the estimated 11.5 residue documents a night (18.2 answers a day in 2025, residue share 0.63) take about 34 minutes.

## 4. Data and operations

**Data.** OPD bulk exports (snapshot 6 Oct 2026; CC BY 4.0, "Source: OpenParlData.ch"), Bern open data, Fedlex, parliament sites, fetched politely (≥ 1.2 s per host and process). `data/` and `results/` are CDLA-Permissive-2.0 and carry OPD's attribution. **Sovereignty and data protection.** No third-party processor by default; besides the public sources, Hugging Face once for the pinned 8B, and a review-model endpoint (Public AI, or the local 8B, where no text leaves the machine) and an alert relay only if the operator sets them. The operator sheet's nDSG row names the personal data (office holders named in public documents), the route that discloses text abroad (Public AI) and the cache retention (default 30 days); for public bodies it recommends rules only or the local 8B. **Operations.** `make run` and the nightly unit run the image read-only, as a non-root uid, with `--cap-drop ALL`; OCR fails loudly (a typed `ocr_failed`, never an empty page); with the pinned deu/fra/ita models all 154/154 P1 documents re-derive from the source PDFs, the 22 Ticino scans included. On standard GitHub runners: **three real network nights**, one with the venv unit (10 Oct) and **two consecutive in the recommended Docker setup** (11 Oct, Actions run 38098822336): 86 min, then 6.6 min (12 bodies re-scanned), 0 alerts; the ledger-status bug night 2 exposed is fixed and covered by a two-night test. A network night refuses to start without a contact URL in `GP_USER_AGENT`. **robots.txt:** we found, audited and fixed our own robots.txt bug; every fetch path now fails closed (disclosure: `docs/incidents.md`).

## 6. Evaluation: ten pre-registrations

**Design.** Each test froze code, prompts and θ by sha256 before any label was committed (P1's hypothesis script followed its labels, D4). Unit (P1, P2, P6): one document's answer date, strict. Arms on identical inputs: **rules**; **pointer**; **cascade** (rules, then the pointer on the residue); **naive** free generation, plain and null-allowed; trivial baselines. **Gold, no human labelling by design:** two blind agent annotators (Claude Opus, Sonnet), a third breaks ties, quotes verified by code, raw files committed; all 96 annotator sessions (P1–P8, P10, the document-parts check, the federal cell) ended before GitHub received their labels (`results/eval/annotation_sessions.json`).
