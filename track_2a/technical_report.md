# Technical report — Gegenprobe: one schema and a converter for Swiss parliamentary answers

**Track 2A — OpenParlData** · Online · Team 444 — Roman (+ Claude Code agents, §7) · Evidence site https://gegenprobe.mi11ion.io · `make run` re-checks the results (§7) · **Maturity:** census and repair lists = *ready for OPD to apply* (needs its choice on the document hash); nightly job = *deployable prototype* (one real night on a CI runner; not yet at OPD); rules-only converter = *tested prototype*; review mode, whole and virtual affairs = *feasibility study*; schema v0.1 = *draft*.

## 1. Summary

**Verdict.** A common schema is feasible with date roles and typed nulls; the converter fills what documents print: the answer date in depth, 7 fields on whole affairs, only the type ready to run unattended (the *unattended bar*: a 95% lower bound ≥ 0.80). Of 17 fields, 12 are shared (meaning tested for 1, the other 11 by definition), 5 shared with a role split, all measured ("answer date" is decided, dispatched or registered), and 3 concepts body-specific; **4 fields are feasible from metadata, 13 partial, 0 need a different concept** (§5). Every value carries its source: page and character span for rule and Apertus dates (an Apertus value is a printed date by construction, located by code), the OPD record for metadata (`route: metadata`); categorical suggestions are document-level, without a span; a missing value is a typed null that says why. By default frozen DE/FR/IT rules run without a model. Outside services: none by default beyond OPD's exports and the parliaments' own sites (fetched politely); review mode adds one model endpoint the operator sets.
**The cross-parliamentary result the schema enables.** OpenParlData (OPD) publishes the affairs of 89 Swiss parliaments with affairs in scope (it scrapes 90). Of 91,268 interventions whose events record a written government answer, **16,749 (18.4%) have no answer text in OPD**. 1,422 of them were answered orally in the sitting (1,016 Valais oral-answer stubs, 406 question-time answers), so the other **15,327 are written answers missing: 17.1% of the 89,846 answered in writing** (2024–25: 1,488 of 12,632, 11.8%), each typed by cause. Bern 2024–25: 567 of 593 written answers lack text, most of them in OPD but unlinked; Neuchâtel 2024–25: 161 of 162 are not in OPD. OPD confirmed the Bern linking bug and that our relink list is the workaround until its fix ships (correspondence, 10 Oct 2026; a published excerpt awaits OPD's consent). Deterministic routes propose repairs for 3,107 of the 16,749; 10,800 written answers need per-parliament source adapters.
**Where Apertus earns its place** (§6). On 62 documents from 15 parliaments no rule was written for (the 29 answer dates come from 7 of them), Apertus finds 24/29 answer dates, the rules 2/29; per parliament it is ahead in 6 and behind in none (sign test p = 0.031). The latest printed date also finds 22/29 but dates 31 of the 33 answerless documents; Apertus dates 4: its value is finding the date and abstaining. On whole affairs it fills the answer date (22/27 → 27/27; held-out replication 20/35 → 31/35, where the unattended default wrote 4/35 wrong dates, 3 of them by a Luzern rule now guarded in the product path). The local 8B finds the dates too (exploratory, 52 documents without Vaud, fast scorer; it ties the latest printed date, 15 vs 14 of 21) but dates many more answerless documents (§3). Another vendor's model relabelled the Claude-made gold blind: date κ 0.80 and 0.87, no registered decision changes (§6).

## 2. Architecture

1. **Census** (`census/`): streams the OPD exports once into a per-affair *answer-link status* (`attached | agenda | parentless | absent | image_only | oral`), the feasibility matrix and a comparability measure.
2. **Repair** (`repair/`): deterministic routes per cause, triple-checked (affair number, answer heading, decision date = OPD's answer event), as lists keyed on OPD ids; a nightly job (`deploy/nightly/`) re-runs them.
3. **Converter** (`convert/`): (i) OPD metadata and events, (ii) frozen DE/FR/IT anchor rules, (iii) the Apertus closed-set pointer on the residue. Modes: `rules_only` (default, no model) and `review` (suggestions with page, span and margin, flagged `needs_review`).
4. **Schema and validator** (`schema/`, `validate/`): Pydantic schema v0.1, normative; every field is a value with provenance `{value, role, page, char_start, char_end, quote, route, rule, model_build, θ, margin}` or a *typed null* (`not_in_document | unlinked | image_only | oral | text_failed | present_unanchored | no_rules_for_body | not_applicable`); `QAItem` pairs question *n* with answer *n* (not yet filled); validation uses the Pydantic models (the JSON Schema cannot express cross-field rules).

## 3. Use of Apertus

**Closed-set pointer** (`convert/pointer.py`, prompts `src/prompts/{de,fr,it}`). The document (first 12,000 characters) and the question go in the user message, the assistant turn is prefilled with a JSON prefix, and `null` and every distinct date printed in the document are scored as continuations by summed log-probability (`prompt_logprobs`). Null if `lp(null) − logsumexp(lp(dates)) > θ`, else the best date, mapped back to its span. θ does not transfer across builds (spike F2: a θ fitted on RLVR scores 0.25 on the final build), so it is fitted per build on dev documents and frozen: θ = 10.40 for the Public AI build (AUROC 0.99, leave-one-out 124/127 [93.3, 99.2]). **Why a pointer** (spikes, `results/report/cited_spikes.json`): asked freely, Apertus dates 15/15 (8B) and 14/15 (70B) Bern interventions that have no answer; a null-allowed prompt fixes the 70B (0/15), not the 8B (14/15).

**Which Apertus, which size.** Unless marked 8B, Apertus numbers are the **70B** weights, which Public AI serves under the name `swiss-ai/apertus-v1.5-8b`. That is inferred, not documented by Public AI: the served fingerprint (`tp8-pp2-…`, a tensor-parallel 8 × pipeline-parallel 2 deployment) and the behaviour point to the 70B (cited: 760/792 greedy outputs byte-identical to the 70B name; MMLU 84.4% vs 70.8% for the v1 8B route). The `-70b` name answers a system message or prefill with Qwen-SEA-LION (3/3), so forced choice goes to the `-8b` name; the response `model` is checked on every call, and every cached P1/P2 answer comes from one served build (`results/eval/served_models.json`, D10). The **8B** is `swiss-ai/Apertus-v1.5-8B` as a community text-only export (`andreasmartin/apertus-v1.5-8b-text`) quantised to 4 bits by a community member (`Colby/apertus-v1.5-8b-text-Q4_K_M-GGUF`, sha256 `a037df8d…` pinned in `llm.py`; not an official Swiss AI release; `docs/runbook.md` "Supply chain"), run with llama.cpp on one 4-vCPU machine, own θ = 16.73 (pre-registered exploratory arms, D2/D3). It cannot return a date that is not printed (by construction). On P1 it is less accurate than the 70B (pointer and cascade McNemar p = 0.0051 and 3.6 × 10⁻⁵; table in §6) and dates 25 of 90 answerless documents (70B: 7); its residue suggestions are right 7/34 (70B 9/16). On the 52 P2 documents reachable from CI (Vaud's 8 answers missing; D2-6), exploratory: rules 33, pointer 35, cascade 33 of 52 (70B pointer 43, cascade 41; 8B vs 70B 2 vs 10 documents, p = 0.039); it finds 15 of 21 answer dates (70B 16, rules 2, the ungated latest printed date 14) but dates 11 of 31 answerless documents (70B 4). Its fast scorer failed both registered parity gates (57.8, then 4.47 nats) and was admitted through a replacement gate written before any 8B arm ran (bf16 reference, 29/30); its preset self-check failed (10/12) (D9). **Recommendation:** `rules_only` unattended; for review suggestions the 8B as the measured sovereign option (a reviewer rejects more), the 70B for accuracy on infrastructure the operator chooses (bf16 weights about 140 GB, e.g. two 80 GB GPUs; arithmetic, not measured). In operation, review mode refuses a served build θ was not fitted on: θ files record the fit's server fingerprints, and the first response from another fingerprint stops the run (exit 3) before any suggestion from it is attached; `docs/runbook.md` "Review mode in operation" gives the refit steps, the owner and the cost.

**Cost** (`results/report/cost_end_to_end.json`, one core, no GPU). Fetch, extraction and checks take about 2.1 s per document, plus 6.9 s per OCR page; the rules process 480 documents per second (0.5 CPU-hours for all 902,754 documents in OPD's export). Review mode adds the pointer on the residue (97 of 154 held-out documents): 3.5 calls and 5,905 billed prompt tokens per residue document on the 70B, USD 0.59 per 1,000 residue documents at Public AI's list price; the 8B takes 180 s per document on 4 vCPUs (692 CPU-seconds, 9.3 GB RAM), 50 machine-hours per 1,000. On all of OPD's export at that residue share (about 569k documents, an upper bound): about 28,400 8B machine-hours. At ten times OPD's volume: about 5 CPU-hours for the rules, about USD 3,357 for review mode on the 70B.

## 4. Data and operations

OPD bulk exports (snapshot 6 Oct 2026; OPD's API robots.txt disallows `/v1/`), Bern open data, Fedlex, source PDFs fetched politely (≥ 1.2 s per host; robots.txt per RFC 9309). `make run` and the nightly unit run the image read-only, as a non-root uid, with `--cap-drop ALL` and `no-new-privileges`; PDFs are capped at 50 MB, each pdftotext/tesseract call at 300 s, each document at 600 s, every hit a typed failure row; failed alerts are re-sent the next night. **One real night** (`results/monitor/night_real_38062530586/`): under systemd on a GitHub-hosted 4-vCPU runner, one network night against OPD's live exports took 83 min (32 of them reading the exports: 6.2 GB of docs/texts streamed, 198 MB of single exports stored; about 50 the first night's triple check of all 2,508 Bern rows), 28 min of CPU, 2,078 MB peak, and reproduced the Bern list on live data (2,459 relink, 49 fetch). It raised no alert; as a first night it had no previous state, so drop and trend alerts could not fire. A two-night run of the recommended Docker setup (night 2 on night 1's state) is scheduled. CI gates both units' `systemd-analyze security` exposure (venv ≤ 2.0, Docker ≤ 5.0; measured 1.2 and 3.9). Inside OPD (`GP_EXPORTS_DIR`) a night with no changed files took 96.8 s on a copied snapshot (0 bytes transferred); one re-scanning three days of changes (80.6% of the docs bytes) about 21.8 min (derived).

## 6. Evaluation: five pre-registrations

**Design.** Five tests: **P1**, 154 held-out documents from parliaments the rules were written for; **P2**, 62 documents from 15 parliaments no rule was written for; **P3**, 29 whole affairs; **P3b**, a pre-registered replication of P3 on 36 fresh affairs; **P4**, affairs rebuilt from minutes. Every test froze code, prompts, θ and the scorer by sha256 before any label was committed: the LOCK commit precedes the label commit by 6 min 0 s (P1), 2 min 28 s (P2), 3 min 37 s (P3), 4 min 3 s (P4) by commit date (P3b: LOCK5 before the draw), an order GitHub's clock confirms (`results/eval/lock_server_times_all.json`). For P1, the frozen rules were committed together with the held-out sample (D12): P1 is held out by protocol (seeded draw, labels after the lock), and P2 is the clean out-of-sample test of the rules. Unit: one document's answer date, strict. Arms on identical inputs: **rules**; **pointer** (Apertus on every document); **cascade** (rules, then the pointer on the residue = every review suggestion accepted); **naive** free generation, plain and null-allowed, same weights.
**Gold, checked by another vendor.** No human labelled a gold value, by design: two blind Claude annotators (Opus, Sonnet), a third breaks ties, quotes verified by code, raw annotations committed. As a check across vendors, `Qwen-SEA-LION-v4-32B-IT` (AI Singapore, Qwen family; neither Anthropic nor Apertus) labelled all 216 P1 and P2 documents blind from the same guide and text (post hoc, D14/D2-7; 216 calls, USD 0.14). Answer present: P1 146/154 (κ 0.89), P2 60/62 (κ 0.93); exact answer date: P1 134/154 (κ 0.80), P2 56/62 (κ 0.87). No registered decision changes when only the documents where both vendors agree are kept or Qwen's labels replace the gold where they differ: P2-H2 is 22 vs 0 under the gold, 21 vs 0 and 23 vs 1. It reads the same extracted text, so it is no human audit (OCR errors stay invisible); disagreements are listed, not adjudicated. P1-H1 (null safety) rests on contested documents: 6 of the 7 values fall on documents OPD types as reports; without the 24 reports it is 1/72 (upper 7.5%), on the documents where both vendors agree 2/84, under Sonnet's labels 5/88. Non-LLM checks: OPD's event matches 37 of 44 checkable P1 dates (5 of the other 7 are 1–5 days apart: decision vs dispatch); Ticino titles 20/20; P3 answer dates 18/21; the 3 P1 gold dates before OPD's submission date are no gold errors (D11); P2: 6 of 29 checkable exactly (6/6 agree), 19/29 face a non-LLM bound. **Statistics** (post hoc, D10): over the 9 registered confirmatory tests all 4 held conclusions survive Holm (P2-H2 3.8 × 10⁻⁶) and BH; the registered lenient metric changes no registered decision; document-level p-values ignore clustering by parliament, so per-parliament results stand beside them. Deviations (`eval/prereg*/DEVIATIONS*.md`) are cited where they apply; D8: the Opus tie-breaker sided with Opus in 36/41 P1 ties, 35 on the recommendation only.

**What the tests say.** Apertus earns its place where layout varies and no rule exists (P2; the answer date in P3/P3b). On P1 the rules alone match rules + Apertus (H2: Branch B), so the default runs no model, and in P4 Apertus added no significant decisions; the rules are not complete there either (P1 recall 47/64, P4 99/120). **Why the pointer form:** the same Apertus asked freely with a null-allowed prompt is as accurate (P2 re-parsed 54/62 vs 53/62; discordant 1 vs 0, p = 1), but every pointer value is a printed date by construction, its page and span located by code (32/32), and its margin orders the review queue (strong band 26/33 right, weak 3/11, post hoc), while the free prompt quotes its date verbatim for 17/32 values and invents dates printed nowhere (1 in P2; the plain prompt 24 in P1).

## 7. Limitations, reproducibility, declarations

Answer dates are the field tested in depth; tables, annexes and images are not extracted; P3/P3b are small (29 + 36 affairs), P4 one commune; no gold set contains federal documents; the 8B was not run on P3/P3b; spans index our own pdftotext/tesseract text (`--opd-text` maps them onto OPD's). `make run` (Docker, CPU, clean checkout) recomputes the census, the Bern join and triple check, sample conversions with validator checks, all scores, hypotheses, the 8B analysis and LOCK files, re-derives the P1 rules arm from the source PDFs and P1/P2 Apertus decisions from the model cache, and re-checks every number in `claims.tsv`.
